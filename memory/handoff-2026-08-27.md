## Updated late in the session

Wipro and Infosys were harvested (financials only, FY2022 to FY2026). Coverage is three companies,
not one. Two things came out of it worth keeping:

- **Capital intensity separates the business models by roughly twenty times.** Sify runs capex at
  1.3 to 3.0 times operating cash flow; Wipro and Infosys run 0.06 to 0.18. The ratio is currency
  free, which is what makes a rupee filer and a dollar filer comparable without an exchange rate.
- **Wipro grew in rupees and shrank in dollars.** FY2023 to FY2026, the rupee line rose while the
  dollar line fell about 10 per cent. The difference is currency, not business. This is why the
  currency rule exists rather than being a precaution, and a test now asserts it.

Segments were not harvested for either name. Both carry `segmentBasis: NOT_HARVESTED` so the gap
is stated rather than implied absent.

## Watch out for

Everything in the traps section of `CLAUDE.md`, but especially these three, which bit during this
session:

- Python `write_text` corrupts UTF-8 unless `encoding="utf-8"` is passed, and a raw apostrophe
  inside a `'''` literal breaks the bash heredoc. Use triple double quotes.
- `next start` survives `pkill`, so kill by port or you will debug a stale build.
- The preview pane will not composite screenshots in this environment. Chart layout was verified
  by measuring text bounding boxes for overlap and out of bounds instead, which caught three real
  defects a screenshot would have shown.
