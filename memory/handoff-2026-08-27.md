## What this session established

The project went from a plan to a working three register product on one company.

- The grid register is complete and is the only register with a real base rate.
- The disclosure register has Sify only: capacity ladder, two graded claims, five refusals.

## The immediate next task

Part 2 of the approved plan: harvest the seven remaining structured companies into
`data/companies/<ticker>.json`.

Per company, roughly seven MCP calls against the FactIQ server:

1. `search_company_filings` with `search_target=coverage` to confirm depth
2. `concept="revenue"`, `metric_class=financial`, `report_type=annual`
3. `concept="Cash flows from used in operating activities"`
4. `concept="Purchase of property plant and equipment classified as investing activities"`
5. `concept="Property plant and equipment"`
6. `search_target=metrics` with `metric_class=segment` to find the segment names
7. `search_target=facts` with the segment filter

Order: WIT and INFY first, because they are Indian and carry the AI services argument. Then EQIX
and DLR, which are the external validity check: their harvested margins must land near their
reported adjusted EBITDA margins or the harvest is being read wrongly. NBIS, APLD and CORZ last,
financials only, since APLD and CORZ carry one and two calls respectively.

Two things to add to Layer 1 while doing it, both already decided:

- A `segmentBasis` field, because Infosys and Wipro segment by industry vertical rather than by
  AI, and the narrative test must not silently compare unlike things.
- The rule that cross company comparison uses margins and ratios only, never absolute currency.
  Three companies file in INR and five in USD.

## Watch out for

Everything in the traps section of `CLAUDE.md`, but especially these three, which bit during this
session:

- Python `write_text` corrupts UTF-8 unless `encoding="utf-8"` is passed, and a raw apostrophe
  inside a `'''` literal breaks the bash heredoc. Use triple double quotes.
- `next start` survives `pkill`, so kill by port or you will debug a stale build.
- The preview pane will not composite screenshots in this environment. Chart layout was verified
  by measuring text bounding boxes for overlap and out of bounds instead, which caught three real
  defects a screenshot would have shown.
