## What exists, honestly

**Complete.** The grid register. Transmission slippage base rate from the Ministry of Power's own
tabling of its late projects: cost weighted 13.7 months against a median of 7, with Power Grid at
8.7 and private developers at 21.5. The pipeline self check asserts that gap holds, so a data
refresh that inverts it fails rather than leaving a stale sentence on the page.

**One company deep.** Sify, because it lists on NASDAQ and therefore files a 20-F while its
Indian-listed peers disclose far less. Nine years of segment economics, data centre margin 40 to
46 per cent, capex above operating cash flow every year from FY2022 to FY2026 by about Rs 2,047
crore cumulative, against a Rs 2,500 crore fresh issue in the draft prospectus. Revenue per
megawatt derived at about Rs 1.1 crore per month from two figures the company disclosed
separately, having declined to give the ratio itself twice.

**Three companies wide.** Wipro and Infosys added for capital intensity only. Sify runs capex at
1.3 to 3.0 times operating cash flow; the two services firms run 0.06 to 0.18. Roughly twentyfold,
and the ratio is currency free, which is what makes a rupee filer comparable to a dollar filer.

**Thin.** The prospectus page carries the offer structure from published summaries, not from the
filed document. The disclosure register is Sify only. There are no per company pages.

## The immediate next task

Harvest Equinix and Digital Realty into `data/companies/`. They matter more as a **check** than as
a comparison: both publish adjusted EBITDA margins, so if the harvested segment margins do not
land near the reported ones, the harvest is being read wrongly. This is the first external
validation the project has had, and it should happen before more names are stacked on an
unvalidated method.

Per company, roughly six MCP calls against the FactIQ server:

0. `search_target=metrics` with a query first. **Concept names drift between filers.** Infosys
   tags recent capex as "Expenditure on property, plant and equipment and intangibles" while Sify
   and Wipro use "Purchase of property plant and equipment classified as investing activities",
   and the Infosys one includes intangibles. Do not assume a concept name.
1. `search_target=coverage` to confirm depth
2. `concept="revenue"`, `metric_class=financial`, `report_type=annual`
3. the cash flow concept the metrics query surfaced
4. the capex concept the metrics query surfaced
5. `search_target=metrics` with `metric_class=segment`, then `search_target=facts` with the
   segment filter

Then normalise into `data/companies/<name>.json` against `CompanyDoc` in `lib/schema.ts`, add it
to the `companies` array in `lib/data.ts`, and extend `lib/diagnostics/diagnostics.test.mjs` with
a margin sanity assertion for that company.

  what they disclose, not a gap to close.

## Environment notes that will bite

- The **Run button executes PowerShell 5.1**, which has no `&&`. Give the user one command per
  fenced block or the play button fails with a parser error. This already caught us out.
- **Large bash heredocs are unreliable in this shell.** Long TSX or Markdown fails unpredictably on
  quoting. Use the Write tool for anything sizeable.
- **`next start` survives `pkill`.** Kill by port with PowerShell `Get-NetTCPConnection` or debug a
  phantom stale build.
- **The preview pane will not composite screenshots.** Verify chart layout by measuring text
  bounding boxes for overlap and out of bounds instead. That method already caught three real
  defects a screenshot would have shown.
- `DATA_GOV_KEY` is only needed by `pipeline/fetch.py`, not at build time. The key was pasted in
  chat during an earlier session and should be rotated.
