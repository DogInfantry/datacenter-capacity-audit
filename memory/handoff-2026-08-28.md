## What changed this session

Two commits.

**`34a8bb3` Check the harvest against two companies that publish the answer.** Equinix and Digital
Realty harvested into `data/companies/`. The point was validation, not coverage: both state an
adjusted EBITDA margin publicly, so the harvest could finally be checked against something outside
the project.

- Equinix reconciles. Rebuilding its own definition, operating income plus depreciation plus stock
  compensation, gives 49.65 per cent for Q2 2025 against the 50 per cent its chief executive
  stated, a gap of 35 basis points, and 47.69 per cent for FY2025 against 49 guided, a gap of 131.
  Tolerances of 150 and 250 basis points were fixed in writing before the check ran.
- Digital Realty cannot be reconciled at all. Its stock compensation is tagged in shares not
  dollars, impairment stops at 2022, transaction costs are never tagged. Its derived margin sits
  inside the guided band in FY2018 at 57.0 per cent and walks to 40.5 by FY2023 against 49.3
  implied. Those years are marked UNVERIFIED and excluded from cross company margin comparison.
- Also fixed: the cross company table was keyed on fiscal year strings, which would have put
  Sify's March year and Equinix's December year in one column. It now keys on the calendar year
  the period ends in.

**`8279571` Measure the refusals instead of illustrating them.** The disclosure register went from
five hand coded refusals from one company to a measured rate across three.

- Across pricing mechanics and cost margin bridge, 2024-01-01 to 2026-07-31, complete topic
  partitions: **Sify refused 2 of 15, Equinix 9 of 20, Digital Realty 9 of 34.**
- The finding inverts the easy thesis. The Indian operator refuses least, the largest global
  operator refuses most. This is the second time the project has looked for an Indian disclosure
  gap and found the opposite, and it is kept for that reason.
- The rate alone would mislead, because Sify is asked 0.43 unit economics questions a call against
  Digital Realty's 0.81. The denominator is on the page beside every rate.
- The sharpest instance also cuts against the thesis. Asked for realization per megawatt in
  October 2024, Sify declined and then said it was in the published financials and "you could do a
  reverse working on that". That reverse working is exactly what `/financials` computes.

## The near miss, and why it matters for the next harvest

The refusal rates were nearly published with a biased denominator. The transcripts tool caps every
response at 50 rows, has no `offset`, and its `truncated` flag reported `false` on a response that
was capped. Six of eight family partitions came back at exactly 50, silently censored and skewed
towards recent quarters.

It was caught because the row count equalled the limit. The fix cost nothing: rows arrive newest
first, and every capped response reached back past 2024-01-01, so the analysis window is complete
even where the full history is not. Per quarter paging would have cost several hundred calls
because `quarter_filter` takes one exact quarter and rejects a year.

**Check `rows returned < limit` on every partitioned harvest. Do not trust `truncated`.**
